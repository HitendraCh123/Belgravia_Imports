ABOUT TICK TOCK TEAS

Rooibos, the wonder plant

At Tick Tock, rooibos runs in the family. Made from a very special plant that comes from just one place, Tick Tock has been perfecting that Tick Tock taste for more than 100 years.

Pronounced ‘roy-boss’, this deliciously smooth, naturally caffeine free tea comes from a plant that grows only in South Africa’s Cederberg coastal mountains. Celebrated as a superfood, ruby-red rooibos is low in tannins, calorie free and packed with powerful antioxidants.

With roots deep in the Cederberg, Tick Tock always seeks to act with the long-term benefit of the rooibos community in mind. By choosing Tick Tock, you’re supporting the wider rooibos family, too.
